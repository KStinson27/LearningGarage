# Sound credits

Vehicle sounds in this folder come from these recordings:

- **Oculus Audio Pack (via Meta Open Sound)** by Oculus VR, LLC, licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: https://github.com/facebookincubator/meta-open-sound. Trimmed and loudness-normalized. Used for: `engine-start` (Car engine starting), `engine-rev` (Car engine revving), `engine-rev-big` (Engine revving twice), `engine-fast` (Engine at high speed), `engine-idle` (Engine running slowly), `train` (Train passing by), `door` (Metal door closing).
